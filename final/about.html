Specific Bands

Bug Hunter

Bug hunter is an alternative/pop rock band consisting of 5 members, the lead singer Bug(his actual name is Cody but he goes by Bug) has a podcast with the Narcissict Cookbook(look below for more info on him) called Jam Mechanics, which I talk more about in Other Audio Products. Bug hunter was formed in Seattle in 2015, it was originally just Bug but later the other members, Moe Hussein, Marcus Alcantara, Jesse Gallaway, and Kyle Hodgkinson joined. They have a lot of more cheerful songs, not to mention a new album and a half coming soon! Some of my favorite songs by them are Disco! In the Panic Room, The Hook(originally created in the podcast), Call out Boy, and Fan Fiction. Bug also has many unreleased demos that he created in the podcast I previously mentioned, I really enjoy a lot of these and play episodes just to listen to them. A few of them are Ichard Noir, the Jam Mechanics Season 4 Theme Song, What Happened to John, Waiting for the Snow to Melt, After the Fact, Fix Itself, Dissin', The lighthouse, Not Your Fault, Kid, The Hook, Kick'n'Spin, and much much more.

The Narcissict Cookbook

The Narcissict Cookbook(also referred to as TNC or Matt Johnston) is a Folk Punk and Spoken word Scottish solo artist. He also cohosts Jam Mechanics with Bug Hunter. I'm unsure as to when he started creating music as he also had a soundcloud account, but the oldest album he released on Spotify was written in 2015 and 2016. TNC has a lot of amazing songs with a large range of emotions, some are really energetic while others are super depressing, he recently just released the second part of two connected albums, which has a more mysterious feel to it, with a lot of lore, telling a full story. Some of my favorite songs by him are Apple, Pardon Me, tweezers, Congnitive Dissonant Blues, and many more, however they're explicit so I won't be putting them here. Other than this, he also has created many amazing demos in Jam mechanics, for example Over the Mountains to Santa Domingo, The Family Dog, Orchids, The Other Boys, Dead Boy, Shut Up and Drive, JMCC, Comparing Yourself to a Sillhouette, The Alpaca Song, Fresh Cement, Get Deep Fried, We're All Enemies Here, Accident?, and a whole lot more.

Not to mention, they're touring together(again) this year, with one being in Seattle on May 20th!
